About AwaazAI

India's most powerful multilingual AI voice generator

Our Mission

AwaazAI was built with one goal: to make professional-quality Indian language voice generation accessible to everyone. Whether you're creating content for YouTube, podcasts, e-learning, or customer service — we've got you covered.

Why AwaazAI?

India speaks in hundreds of dialects and languages. Most TTS tools treat Indian languages as an afterthought. We built AwaazAI from the ground up for Indian creators, with deep support for Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Punjabi, Odia, and English (Indian accent).

Our Technology

We use Sarvam AI's cutting-edge bulbul:v2 model — specifically tuned for Indian languages with natural prosody, correct phonetics, and expressive delivery. Our platform adds smart text splitting, audio stitching, and a studio-quality interface on top.
