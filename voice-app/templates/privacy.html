Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when creating an account (name, email), and usage data (text input, language, speaker selection). We do not collect payment card data — this is handled securely by Razorpay.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the Service
  • To enforce daily credit limits
  • To send transactional emails (verification, password reset)
  • To provide support

3. Voice History

Generated audio is stored in your account history so you can replay and download it. You can delete individual items or your entire history at any time.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Text inputs are sent to Sarvam AI's API for processing — see Sarvam AI's privacy policy for their data practices.

5. Cookies

We use session cookies to keep you logged in and a localStorage key to remember your theme preference. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

6. Data Retention

Account data is retained until you delete your account. Voice history is retained until deleted by you or after 90 days on the free plan.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us at privacy@awaazai.in to exercise these rights.

8. Security

We use industry-standard security practices including password hashing (bcrypt), HTTPS, and session management. No system is 100% secure — report any vulnerabilities to our security team.

9. Contact

For privacy questions: privacy@awaazai.in
